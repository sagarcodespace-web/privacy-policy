Loopwise Followup CRM

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Loopwise Followup mobile application (the "App"), developed and operated by Loopwise Technologies ("we", "us", or "our"). Loopwise Followup is an enterprise CRM and field service delivery platform built to manage customer inquiries, quotation milestones, and on-site engineering deliveries.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is collected, used, and stored responsibly. This Privacy Policy details the types of data we collect and how we safeguard it.

1. Information We Collect

In order to provide our CRM and field execution services, the App may collect the following information:

  • User Account Information: Name, work email address, company title, and role (Sales Executive, Service Delivery Engineer, Regional Manager, or Client) collected during registration or login.
  • Customer & Inquiry Details: Business contact names, phone numbers, email addresses, site factory addresses, and commercial inquiry notes entered by users to facilitate business transactions.
  • Photos and Attachments: Images captured or uploaded by users (such as rough notebook scans of inquiry requirements, proof of delivery sign-offs, or site installation photos).
  • Device Identifiers & Push Tokens: Push notification tokens and device permission states required to deliver real-time notifications for overdue follow-ups and delivery SLA milestones.

2. Device Permissions Required

The App requests standard Android and iOS device permissions only when necessary for core features:

  • Camera & Photo Library: Used strictly to allow sales reps and field engineers to attach rough call notes, equipment pictures, and site commissioning reports to tickets.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to deliver audible alerts, vibration, and banner reminders when customer follow-up dates arrive or delivery deadlines are approaching.
  • Phone & WhatsApp Linking: Used to launch the device's native dialer or WhatsApp client with contextual inquiry templates upon explicit user action.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:

  • Authenticate user accounts and enforce role-based access controls.
  • Maintain real-time operational records of customer inquiries, Purchase Orders (POs), and site commissioning statuses.
  • Generate executive analytics, win-conversion rates, and team performance dashboards.
  • Send scheduled in-app and system-level reminders for follow-ups.

4. Data Storage and Security

All user credentials, inquiry records, and activity logs are stored securely using industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. Cloud data storage is hosted on Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication & Storage, adhering to strict enterprise security standards.

5. Third-Party Services

The App utilizes trusted third-party cloud infrastructure providers:

  • Google Firebase: User authentication, cloud database, and secure asset storage.
  • Expo / EAS (Expo Application Services): Application build infrastructure and push notification delivery.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal or business data to third-party advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain ticket and profile data only for as long as necessary to fulfill operational CRM purposes or as required by your business account administrator.

Account & Data Deletion:
You may request the deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by contacting our support team at support@loopwise.com. Upon verification, your account and stored personal identifiers will be permanently removed.

7. Children's Privacy

Loopwise Followup is an enterprise business application intended for professional use by adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
